## 1. What this notice covers

This notice explains what personal data MaurMaket collects, why we use it, who we share it with, and the controls you have. It applies when you use the MaurMaket app and any related website. It does not cover third-party services you reach through links, such as MonCash or NatCash, which have their own policies.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account data:** name, email, phone number, password (stored only as a secure hash), and the profile details you choose to add, such as bio, avatar, and public username.
- **Verification data:** for sellers, identity documents (CIN photos) submitted for verification. Documents are checked and then deleted; we keep only the verification result and a limited audit record.
- **Transaction data:** orders, items, prices, meetup or delivery details, reviews, payout records, and messages between buyers and sellers.
- **Technical data:** device and app information needed to run the service, sign-in activity, and push notification tokens.
- **Optional data:** your location only when you explicitly enable map discovery or use map features; anything you report to support.

## 3. Why we use it

We use your data to provide the marketplace: create your account, show listings, process orders and payments, arrange meetups and delivery, hold and release funds, send service notifications, prevent fraud and abuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data.

## 4. Payments

Payments are handled by MonCash (through MonCashConnect) and, for NatCash, directly between buyer and seller. We receive payment status and references needed to run orders — we never see or store your MonCash PIN, and we do not store card numbers. NatCash transfers between users are not processed by MaurMaket.

## 5. Who we share it with

We share data with service providers that host our database, send emails and push notifications, process payments, and serve maps — only what each needs to do its job. We may disclose data when required by law or to protect the safety of users. Sellers see the buyer information needed to fulfill an order (name, contact, and meetup or delivery details); other users see only your public profile.

## 6. Location and maps

Public seller discovery on the map is off by default. If you enable it, we publish only an approximate area you chose yourself — never your home address or live device location. Exact meetup points and delivery addresses are private to the order participants. We do not track your location in the background.

## 7. Your controls

From the app's Data & privacy settings you can: see a summary of the data we hold, download an export of your data, correct your profile information, request deletion of your account, and turn off in-app search discoverability. You can also manage device permissions and notification preferences. Some records (orders, security events, audit logs) must be kept after account closure for legal and integrity reasons, as described in the retention table in the app.

## 8. Retention

We keep account data while your account is active. Messages, reviews, and notifications are kept for their stated retention periods in the app, security history for 12 months, and staff access logs for identity documents for 12 months. Payment and order records are kept as required for accounting and dispute handling. When retention ends, data is deleted or anonymized.

## 9. Security and contact

We protect data with encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls, and audit logs for sensitive staff actions. No system is perfectly secure — if we ever have a data breach that affects you, we will notify you as required by law. Questions, requests, or complaints: contact support through this site (opening soon) or the in-app Help & Support screen. Material changes to this notice will be shown in the app for your confirmation.
